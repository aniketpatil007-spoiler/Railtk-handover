Reservation Office Checklist

Carry these physical documents to the Chief Reservation Supervisor at the nearest Railway Reservation Office (PRS counter):

  • Signed Request Letter (printed using this assistant).
  • Electronic Reservation Slip (ERS) printout of the confirmed e-ticket.
  • Original Photo ID of the booked passenger (Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID).
  • Original Photo ID of the family member traveling instead.
  • Proof of Family Relationship (Ration Card, Passport, Birth/Marriage Certificate).
Railway Office Approval: Railway staff evaluate and decide whether to approve the transfer. Resale or transfer outside eligible family is prohibited under the Indian Railways Act.
